# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is researchers, potential collaborators, professors, and employers who want a concise view of Dragos Sorescu's applied machine-learning work. Visitors should be able to understand his research interests, inspect selected projects, review his experience, and find a direct way to contact him without navigating a large portfolio.

## Product Purpose

This personal research site presents Dragos as a Research Assistant at Durham College's AI Hub and a machine-learning developer who can carry ideas from experiments into working systems. Success means a visitor quickly understands his focus, opens a relevant project or résumé, and feels comfortable reaching out.

## Positioning

Dragos combines applied AI research with hands-on experience building complete machine-learning and computer-vision systems.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: contact Dragos by email.
- Secondary CTA: review his résumé or GitHub work.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Dragos builds practical AI systems from research through deployment.
- Belief ladder: Dragos works on credible applied research; he has built real machine-learning systems; he understands both experimentation and implementation; he is open to collaboration.
- Proof on hand: public GitHub repositories, a résumé PDF, the Durham College AI Hub research role, and project descriptions drawn from the existing portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Focused, direct, and thoughtful. The site should feel like a well-edited academic profile: quiet enough for the work to lead, current enough to show care, and approachable rather than institutional.

## Anti-references

The site must not resemble a templated SaaS landing page, a corporate résumé builder, a hacker terminal, or an effects-heavy creative portfolio. Avoid ornamental gradients, generic technology icons, oversized marketing copy, and animation that distracts from the content.

## Design Principles

1. **Let the work lead.** Give research, projects, and experience more visual weight than decoration.
2. **Make scanning effortless.** Use a short page, descriptive headings, clear dates, and compact project summaries.
3. **Keep evidence one action away.** GitHub repositories, the résumé, LinkedIn, and email should always be easy to reach.
4. **Use restraint as a point of view.** Strong typography, exact spacing, and thin rules should carry the black-and-white design.
5. **Stay easy to maintain.** Keep the implementation to semantic HTML and CSS so Dragos can update projects directly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, readable line lengths, strong contrast, descriptive link labels, generous touch targets, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
